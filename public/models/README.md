# Models

## starmind.glb

The spacecraft drawn on the globe. Self-authored for this project and released under the repository's MIT
license. It is generated by `scripts/models/build-starmind.mjs` (run `node scripts/models/build-starmind.mjs`);
the textures (solar cells, phased-array tiles, gold MLI) are drawn by that script, not taken from elsewhere.

It is **illustrative**. SpaceX has not published Starmind's geometry. The model only honours the published totals
from the spacex.com Starmind sheet (see `docs/research/reference-values.md`):

| Quantity | Value | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Wingspan | 75 m | sourced |
| Height | 30 m | sourced |
| Radiator area | 160 m² (two 4 m × 20 m panels) | sourced total, panel shape estimated |
| Solar array area | 840 m² (two 32 m × 13.125 m wings) | derived: 210 kW ÷ 250 W/m², wing shape estimated |
| Bus 8 m × 6 m × 8 m, 1.5 m booms | | estimate |

Axes and attitude: +X along the velocity, +Y zenith (the phased array faces nadir), +Z = X × Y. The globe keeps the
craft nadir-pointing and turns both wings about Z towards the Sun (single-axis tracking, so at high beta angles the
cells are not square to the Sun). On the globe the model is enlarged ×5,000 unless "True scale" is on.
